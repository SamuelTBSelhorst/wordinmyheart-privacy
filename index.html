Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

This app ("Word in My Heart") is a study and memorisation tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Email address (for account creation and login)
  • User ID and authentication data
  • Audio recordings created by users for memorisation practice
  • App usage data (to improve performance and features)
  • Subscription information and purchase status

How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Store and manage memorisation recordings
  • Manage subscriptions and Premium features
  • Improve app performance and user experience

Subscriptions and Payments

Word in My Heart offers optional subscriptions that provide access to Premium features. Subscriptions are processed through the Apple App Store and Google Play Billing.

Payment information is handled directly by Apple and Google. Word in My Heart does not collect, store, or have access to your payment details.

RevenueCat is used to manage subscription status and provide access to purchased features. RevenueCat may process information such as subscription status and purchase information according to their privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

This app uses third-party services including:

  • Supabase (authentication and data storage)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Apple App Store (in-app purchases and subscription payments)
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases and subscription payments)

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Storage

Your data may be securely stored on cloud servers provided by third-party services. We take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Data Deletion

We will process requests and delete associated personal data where applicable.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy
