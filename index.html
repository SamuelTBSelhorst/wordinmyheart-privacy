Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Word in My Heart ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a Bible study and memorisation application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: such as your email address and user ID when you create an account.
  • Authentication information: information required to securely authenticate your account.
  • Audio recordings: Audio recordings created using the App's recording feature are stored locally on your device and are not collected or uploaded by us.
  • App content: information you create or save in the App, such as memorisation notes, passages, progress, and related content.
  • Usage information: information about how you use the App, where collected, such as app activity and information used to maintain and improve the App.
  • Subscription information: information relating to your Premium subscription, including subscription status and purchase-related information.

Microphone and Audio Recordings

Word in My Heart requests access to your device's microphone so that you can record your voice while practising Bible memorisation.

Audio recordings are created only when you choose to use the recording function. Your audio recordings are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

We do not access, collect, transmit, or sell your audio recordings.

How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App;
  • Create and manage user accounts;
  • Synchronise your memorisation data across supported devices;
  • Store and provide access to your audio recordings;
  • Track memorisation progress and provide App features;
  • Manage Premium subscriptions and entitlements;
  • Provide customer support;
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse; and
  • Improve the performance, reliability, and functionality of the App.

Subscriptions and Payments

Word in My Heart offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions that provide access to Premium features.

Subscriptions purchased through the iOS version of the App are processed through Apple's App Store and Apple's in-app purchase system. Subscriptions purchased through the Android version of the App are processed through Google Play Billing.

Word in My Heart does not collect or store your credit card, debit card, or other payment card details. Payment information is handled by Apple or Google through their respective payment systems.

RevenueCat is used to manage subscription information and determine whether a user has an active Premium entitlement. RevenueCat may process information such as an anonymous or pseudonymous app user identifier, subscription status, product information, transaction information, and other information necessary to provide subscription management services.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to provide certain functionality within the App. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase: used for authentication and cloud data storage.
  • RevenueCat: used to manage subscriptions and Premium entitlements.
  • Apple: used for iOS App Store distribution and in-app purchases.
  • Google: used for Android App distribution and Google Play Billing.

Data Storage

Account information, memorisation data, and other information that you choose to synchronise may be stored using third-party cloud services, including Supabase.

Audio recordings created using the App are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or audio recordings.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to provide the App's functionality, including authentication, cloud storage, subscription management, and payment processing.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, or the security of the App.

Data Retention

We retain account information and App data for as long as necessary to provide the App and its functionality, or as otherwise required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

When you request deletion of your account, we will take reasonable steps to delete associated personal information and App data, subject to information that we may be required or permitted to retain by law.
